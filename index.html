Featured Games

Larger projects I led, with gameplay footage.

Koitopia: Koi Simulation

A koi simulation where one fish model becomes endless unique fish. Genes drive each fish's colours, body and behaviour, and pass to the next generation with small mutations, so siblings look related but never identical.

Every fish is rendered with five Shader Graph layers I wrote:

  • Base layer: body colour that shifts with age, health and genetics.
  • Three pigment layers: project custom pigment sheets for detailed, adjustable patterns.
  • Detail layer: scales, metallic sheen and fine lighting.
Shader Graph Genetics system 3D Solo lead
